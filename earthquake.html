Earthquake Safety

Earthquakes can happen without warning. Prepare your home and learn the recommended protective action.

Before

  • Secure shelves, cabinets and heavy objects.
  • Identify safe spots away from glass and objects that may fall.
  • Practice DROP, COVER and HOLD ON.
  • Keep shoes, flashlight and emergency supplies accessible.

During

  • DROP to your hands and knees.
  • COVER your head and neck and take cover under sturdy furniture if available.
  • HOLD ON until the shaking stops.
  • If outdoors, move away from buildings, trees and power lines.

After

  • Expect aftershocks and be ready to protect yourself again.
  • Check for injuries and hazards when safe.
  • Do not enter damaged buildings.
  • Follow PHIVOLCS and local authority advisories.
