Volcanic Activity and Ashfall

Volcanic hazards may include ashfall, volcanic gases, lava flows and other dangerous phenomena. The hazards depend on the volcano and its current activity.

Before

  • Know whether your community is within a hazard zone.
  • Prepare masks, goggles and essential supplies for ashfall.
  • Know official evacuation routes and shelters.
  • Follow PHIVOLCS updates.

During ashfall

  • Stay indoors when possible and close doors and windows.
  • Use a well-fitting mask and eye protection if you must go outside.
  • Protect water and food from ash contamination.
  • Follow instructions about roof cleaning and structural safety.

Evacuation

  • Evacuate immediately when ordered.
  • Do not enter permanent danger zones.
  • Bring your go-bag and follow designated routes.
  • Return only when authorities say it is safe.

Official information: PHIVOLCS.
