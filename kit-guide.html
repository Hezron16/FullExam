Emergency Kit Guide

Prepare supplies for your household, considering the people who live with you and the hazards in your area.

Water and food

Store safe drinking water and non-perishable food. Plan for each household member and follow local health guidance for safe storage.

Health and first aid

Include a first-aid kit, prescription medicines, copies of prescriptions and needed medical or assistive supplies.

Light and communication

Pack a flashlight, spare batteries, a radio and a charged power bank.

Documents and money

Keep copies of IDs, emergency contacts and essential records in a waterproof pouch. Keep some cash in small bills.

Clothing and hygiene

Pack comfortable clothes, sturdy footwear, rain protection, soap, wipes and personal hygiene items.

Special household needs

Consider infants, older adults, persons with disabilities and pets. Pack supplies that are appropriate for them.

Check your kit regularly. Replace expired food, water, batteries and medicines according to their labels and professional advice.
