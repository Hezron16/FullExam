Flood Safety

Flooding may happen after prolonged rain, overflowing rivers or storm surge. Follow local warnings and evacuation instructions.

Before

  • Know if your home or route is flood-prone.
  • Identify a safe route to higher ground with local officials.
  • Move documents and essential items to a safer place.
  • Prepare a waterproof go-bag.

During

  • Move to higher ground when advised.
  • Never walk, swim or drive through floodwater.
  • Keep away from drainage canals and moving water.
  • Switch off electricity only if it is safe to do so and follow official guidance.

After

  • Return only when authorities say it is safe.
  • Avoid floodwater that may be contaminated.
  • Do not touch electrical equipment that has been wet.
  • Clean and disinfect affected areas safely.
