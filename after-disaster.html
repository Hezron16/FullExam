After a Disaster

Recovery can take time. Protect your health and safety while following official advice.

Check for immediate danger

Stay away from damaged buildings, unstable slopes, floodwater and downed power lines. Report hazards to authorities.

Use safe food and water

Follow local health advisories about drinking water, food safety, cleanup and sanitation.

Get reliable assistance

Contact your barangay, local DRRMO or recognized relief organizations for verified information and support.

Support your community

Check on neighbors when safe, share verified information and respect the needs and privacy of affected people.
