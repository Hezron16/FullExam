Community Preparedness

Disaster resilience grows when neighbors, schools, local leaders and organizations plan together.

Map local risks

Work with local officials to understand hazard-prone areas, safe routes and community resources.

Include everyone

Consider children, older adults, persons with disabilities, pregnant people, and households with limited resources.

Share trusted information

Use clear language and accessible formats. Share verified advisories and correct misinformation respectfully.

Practice and improve

Participate in community drills and review what worked after exercises or emergencies.
