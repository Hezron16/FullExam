Fire Safety

Fire prevention and a practiced escape plan can help protect everyone at home, school and work.

Prevent

  • Keep matches and lighters away from children.
  • Do not overload electrical outlets.
  • Keep cooking areas attended and clear of flammable items.
  • Check electrical cords and appliances for damage.

If fire happens

  • Alert people nearby and leave using a safe exit.
  • Stay low if there is smoke.
  • Do not use elevators.
  • Once outside, stay outside and call emergency services.

Plan ahead

  • Identify at least two possible exits where available.
  • Choose an outdoor meeting place.
  • Practice the escape plan with everyone.
  • Ask the local fire station about fire-safety education.
