Before a Disaster

Preparedness starts before a warning is issued. Small steps taken early can make a household more ready.

Know your risk

Ask your barangay or local DRRMO about hazards, evacuation centers and safe routes in your area.

Make a household plan

Choose meeting places, emergency contacts and a way to communicate if family members are separated.

Prepare supplies

Pack essential food, water, medicines, light, communication tools and important documents.

Practice together

Discuss the plan and practice age-appropriate drills. Include people who may need extra support.
