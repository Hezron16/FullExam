During a Disaster

Stay informed, protect yourself and follow instructions from authorized responders.

Use trusted information

Listen to official government announcements and local DRRMO instructions. Be careful about unverified social-media posts.

Protect people first

Move away from immediate danger. Follow hazard-specific safety guidance and help others only when it is safe.

Communicate clearly

Use your household communication plan. Keep messages short and preserve phone battery when power is limited.

Follow evacuation orders

Use designated routes and bring essential supplies. Do not return to restricted areas until authorities allow it.

The safest action depends on the hazard and your location. Follow instructions from local emergency officials.
