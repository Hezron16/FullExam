Landslide Safety

Landslides may occur on steep slopes, especially after prolonged or intense rainfall. Know local hazards and heed evacuation notices.

Before

  • Ask local officials whether your area is landslide-prone.
  • Notice changes such as new cracks or unusual water flow and report them.
  • Know evacuation routes and safe locations.
  • Monitor weather and local announcements.

During a warning

  • Leave promptly when authorities advise evacuation.
  • Keep away from slopes, river channels and areas identified as dangerous.
  • Do not cross an active landslide area.
  • Help household members who need assistance, if safe.

After

  • Stay away from the affected slope; additional movement may occur.
  • Do not return until authorities declare the area safe.
  • Report damaged roads, utilities or new ground movement.

Check hazard and weather information from relevant government agencies and your local DRRMO.
