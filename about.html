About HandaPH

A simple preparedness guide designed for Filipino households and communities.

Our purpose

HandaPH supports disaster awareness by organizing basic preparedness information in one easy-to-use website. It encourages people to plan before a hazard happens and to follow trusted authorities during emergencies.

Our SDG connection

This project supports UN Sustainable Development Goal 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities, especially the idea of making communities safer and more resilient to disasters.

Who can use it?

Students, parents, workers, caregivers, older adults and community members. The pages use plain language, clear headings, readable contrast and responsive layouts.

What HandaPH can and cannot do

  • It can provide general learning materials and planning checklists.
  • It cannot send alerts, locate evacuation centers in real time or replace local disaster officials.
  • Always check official sources for current warnings and instructions.
