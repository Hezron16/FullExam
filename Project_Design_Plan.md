# HandaPH — Project Design Plan

## 1. Theme of the Website
**Chosen SDG:** UN Sustainable Development Goal 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities.

**Philippine issue:** Filipino communities experience natural and human-made hazards such as typhoons, floods, earthquakes, fires, landslides and volcanic activity. People may not know where to find clear preparedness information or how to organize a household plan.

HandaPH is a disaster-preparedness information website that presents basic guidance in simple language and encourages households to prepare before emergencies happen.

## 2. Objective of the Website
The website aims to help Filipino families and community members understand common hazards, prepare an emergency kit, create a family communication plan, and learn general safety actions before, during and after a disaster. It also directs users to official government information sources.

## 3. Target Audience or Demographic
The target audience includes Filipino students, parents, workers, caregivers and community members. The design considers users with different levels of digital experience, mobile-device users, older adults and people who may need accessible text and clear navigation.

## 4. Specific Pages and Page Content
1. **Home (`index.html`)** — introduces HandaPH and links to the main preparedness tools.
2. **About (`about.html`)** — explains the purpose, SDG connection, intended users and limitations.
3. **Choose a Hazard (`hazards.html`)** — provides links to six hazard guides.
4. **My Checklist (`checklist.html`)** — interactive checklist for emergency supplies and household needs.
5. **Kit Guide (`kit-guide.html`)** — explains essential emergency-kit categories.
6. **Family Plan (`family-plan.html`)** — lets users add emergency contacts and household members on the page and plan meeting places.
7. **Typhoon (`typhoon.html`)** — general before, during and after typhoon guidance.
8. **Flood (`flood.html`)** — general flood preparedness and safety guidance.
9. **Earthquake (`earthquake.html`)** — DROP, COVER and HOLD ON and other earthquake reminders.
10. **Fire (`fire.html`)** — fire prevention and escape-plan guidance.
11. **Landslide (`landslide.html`)** — awareness, warning and evacuation reminders.
12. **Volcanic Activity (`volcano.html`)** — ashfall and evacuation reminders.
13. **Before a Disaster (`before-disaster.html`)** — household preparation, risk awareness and practice.
14. **During a Disaster (`during-disaster.html`)** — trusted information, protective actions and evacuation.
15. **After a Disaster (`after-disaster.html`)** — safety, sanitation and community recovery reminders.
16. **Community Preparedness (`community.html`)** — inclusive community planning and shared readiness.
17. **Learning Videos (`videos.html`)** — five original short MP4 learning clips.
18. **References and Credits (`references.html`)** — APA-style references and media credits.

## 5. Layout / Wireframe
The same layout is used across the website:
- **Header:** HandaPH name, tagline and navigation menu.
- **Main content:** page title, short introduction, then content arranged in cards, sections or a two-column layout. Hazard pages use an illustration followed by before/during/after sections.
- **Footer:** project name, educational-use note and reference link.

The home page uses a two-column hero section with introduction and illustration, followed by three cards. The hazard selection page uses a responsive card grid. The checklist uses grouped checkboxes. The family plan uses labeled forms and lists. The video page uses a responsive video-card grid.

## 6. Color Scheme and Explanation
- **Navy blue (#102B50):** header and footer; communicates trust and provides strong contrast with white text.
- **Blue (#1D5BA6):** links and interactive elements; supports clear visual navigation.
- **Pale blue (#EEF5FC):** page background; keeps the interface calm and readable.
- **Yellow (#FFD04A):** buttons and important highlights; makes actions easy to notice.
- **White (#FFFFFF):** content cards; separates sections and improves readability.

The layout uses readable font sizes, visible keyboard focus, responsive grids and text alternatives for images. Color is not the only way information is communicated.

## 7. Technologies
HTML5, CSS3 and basic JavaScript only. No Bootstrap or other CSS framework is used.
