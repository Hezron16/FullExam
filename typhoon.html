Typhoon Safety

Typhoons can bring strong winds, intense rainfall, flooding and storm surge. Prepare early and monitor official weather bulletins.

Before

  • Monitor PAGASA bulletins and local government announcements.
  • Prepare a go-bag, medicines and drinking water.
  • Clear drains safely and secure loose outdoor objects.
  • Know whether your area may be affected by flooding or storm surge.

During

  • Stay indoors and away from windows during strong winds.
  • Do not go outside during a lull; conditions can change quickly.
  • Evacuate when instructed by local officials.
  • Avoid floodwater and downed electrical wires.

After

  • Wait for official advice before returning to affected areas.
  • Avoid damaged buildings, fallen wires and floodwater.
  • Use safe water and follow health advisories.
  • Report urgent hazards to local authorities.

Official information: PAGASA.
